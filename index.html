FIELD MANUAL

Destroy the other tanks to win a round. Each commander gets one offensive action per turn. Equipment, construction and movement leave the shot available.

  • Aim: 0° fires right, 90° fires up, 180° fires left. Power is limited to health × 10. Azimuth moves the shot across the battlefield depth.
  • Cameras: F centers and zooms onto your tank. E opens 3D exploration; drag the battlefield to orbit, scroll to zoom, and use WASD to pan. Orbit, Tilt and Zoom sliders also work. R resets the full 480-meter map. A shot launched in 3D gets a brief tank-side launch view, then switches automatically to tactical flight with a solid trail and dashed remaining arc.
  • V: enter the barrel camera. T: toggle the predicted arc. Arrow keys adjust aim; Space fires. The arc predicts the initial shot, not secondary effects or magnetic defenses.
  • Weapons: choose a payload and press Fire. Select an accessory or building and press Use / Deploy. Drones target the nearest enemy, or the coordinate entered in Target X (0–480).
  • Terrain: craters cause falls. Dirt buries tanks and inflicts pressure damage; Riot Bomb clears it safely. Diggers collapse trenches. This heightfield cannot form caves or overhangs.
  • Survive: shields absorb damage. Parachutes deploy automatically. Batteries restore 45 HP. A CIWS intercepts one shot each owner turn. Repair depots restore 15 HP and shield.
  • Economy: every round pays $800, plus $600 to the winner, then 5% interest. Free-market prices fluctuate with purchases.
  • Online: the host simulates the battle and sends authoritative snapshots. A disconnected commander becomes a bot. If the host leaves, reconnect or start a local game.
