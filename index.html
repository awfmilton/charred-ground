FIELD MANUAL

Destroy the other tanks to win a round. Shared Action Economy: each commander gets 2 actions on their first 3 personal turns (setup phase) and 1 action from their 4th personal turn onward. Driving, firing, deploying a structure/shield/drone/satellite, calling an orbital strike, or launching an ASAT missile each costs 1 action. Aiming is free; invalid actions cost 0.

  • Aim & Flight: 0° fires right, 90° fires up, 180° fires left. Power is capped at HP × 10. There are no predicted aim or remaining-flight arcs—judge wind and elevation using actual projectile flight history trails.
  • Cameras: F focuses your tank, E opens 3D exploration, V enters Gunner optics, R resets tactical view. Shots fired from 3D views show a brief launch camera before returning to tactical flight with live trail history.
  • Piloted 3D Drones: Human commanders pilot FPV Kamikaze and Strike Attack Drones from an onboard 3D camera using W/S or ↑/↓ (pitch), A/D or ←/→ (yaw/bank), Q/E (throttle), and Space (detonate FPV or fire up to 3 anti-tank rounds per Strike Attack Drone sortie). Wind pushes drones and battery duration is bounded.
  • Orbital & Anti-Drone Systems: Deploy Orbital Laser Satellites and call downward laser strikes (blocked by canopies/structures and absorbed by shields). Deploy Anti-Satellite Silos (1 action) and launch ASAT interceptors on a later action. Anti-Drone Net Launchers and Flak Shotguns automatically engage hostile drones in range.
  • Terrain: craters cause falls. Dirt buries tanks and inflicts pressure damage; Riot Bomb clears it safely. Diggers collapse trenches. This heightfield cannot form caves or overhangs.
  • Survive: shields absorb damage. Parachutes deploy automatically. Batteries restore 45 HP. A CIWS intercepts one shot each owner turn. Repair depots restore 15 HP and shield.
  • Economy: every round pays $800, plus $600 to the winner, then 5% interest. Free-market prices fluctuate with purchases.
  • Online: the host simulates the battle and sends authoritative snapshots. A disconnected commander becomes a bot. If the host leaves, reconnect or start a local game.
