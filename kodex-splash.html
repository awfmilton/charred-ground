CHARRED GROUND

Tactical artillery on an open battlefield. The command server sleeps between battles to keep the lights cheap, so the first visit takes a minute or two while it warms up.

  • Pick your angle and power, and trust the wind.
  • Drive, buy gear between rounds, and leave a crater.
  • Play local, against AI commanders, or online with friends.
